Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from asking Perplexity or ChatGPT?

AI language models generate plausible-sounding text, but they hallucinate facts, confuse different people with identical names, miss paywalled or gated podcast appearances, and provide dead links. Our desk uses frontier AI models for rapid broad discovery, but every claim is cross-referenced, checked against primary sources, and verified by an analyst before you receive it.

What information do you need from me to start?

Just the person’s full name and current organization or title. If you have an upcoming meeting, sharing the context (e.g., "Exploring executive coaching for their leadership team" or "Interviewing them on my podcast") helps us surface the exact conversational angles most relevant to your goal.

How quickly can I receive a brief?

Standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours. If you have an urgent, short-notice C-suite meeting booked for the next morning, we offer an expedited 12-hour turnaround for an additional $50 rush fee.

What is the Weekly Network Radar in the retainer?

For coaches managing relationships with dozens of board members, high-profile clients, and prospective sponsors, staying informed is impossible. We monitor your selected list of 15–20 key people weekly. Every Monday morning, you get a clean summary of only the notable events that happened in their world over the last 7 days—giving you authentic, timely reasons to reach out and stay top-of-mind.
